# Harcov Dam, Liberec

![ImgName](https://dynamic-media-cdn.tripadvisor.com/media/photo-o/19/f5/f7/02/prehradni-nadrz-harcov.jpg?w=1200&h=1200&s=1)
> A historic dam in Liberec, Czech Republic

## About
Harcov Dam is located on Harcovský Brook in the city of Liberec. It was built between 1902 and 1904 and is associated with the dam-design principles of Otto Intze, an engineer known for his work on water-storage dams in Central Europe. The structure is a masonry gravity dam: its weight helps resist the pressure of the water stored behind it.

The reservoir is part of the local water-management system and has also become a familiar feature of the city’s landscape. People visit the area to walk, relax by the water, and enjoy the surrounding greenery. Like other reservoirs, Harcov Dam must be managed carefully so that it can fulfil its water-management role while protecting people and infrastructure downstream.

The dam has undergone major modernisation work to improve its condition and its ability to cope with demanding flood situations. Its history illustrates how water infrastructure can serve practical engineering needs while also becoming an important recreational and visual feature of a city.
